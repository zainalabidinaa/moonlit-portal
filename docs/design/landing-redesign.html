Questions

Before you sign up.

Something else? Write to us from the Support page and a person answers.

Do I need to configure anything?

No. The catalog, artwork and stream ranking are managed for you. Studio subscribers can optionally add their own sources, but nothing requires it.

Which devices does it run on?

Mac today, iPhone and iPad through the TestFlight beta, and any modern browser. Apple TV and Windows apps are in development.

How do profiles work?

Each person at home gets their own profile with a separate library, continue-watching and recommendations. Spotlight includes six, Studio has no limit.

What does an invite code do?

An admin can invite friends and family onto their household catalog at no cost. You get a normal profile and library, without a subscription.

Can I cancel?

Yes, from Billing at any time. Your profiles stay until the end of the paid period.

Redesign notes

Same page structure in all three concepts. Use the switcher to compare palette and type. The layout fixes below apply to all of them.

Three concepts

Ember (recommended)Keeps the current warm-black and orange identity but uses the orange in one place per section instead of everywhere. Mixed-case Bricolage Grotesque headlines, no glow on text, no marquees.
MoonriseCool night sky with moon-gold accent and an editorial serif. Reads more premium and calmer. Furthest from the current brand.
MatineeLight, paper-toned page with the app mockups kept dark so they pop. Distinct from every other streaming site, and better for daytime reading and email screenshots.

What was not working

  1. Every heading was uppercase. Twelve shouting headings in a row flatten the hierarchy. Now only the app-mock titles use caps.
  2. Orange everywhere. Glow text-shadows, glowing numbers, glowing borders and two marquees competed with the CTA. Accent is now the CTA, the eyebrow and the auto-picked stream.
  3. Empty mockups. Poster rows were blank gradient rectangles and some cards fell back to "no image". Every tile now carries a title, badge or progress bar so it reads as a real app.
  4. Filler sections. "By the numbers" (∞ / 6 / 0), the ghost outline wordmark and the second marquee said nothing. Replaced with a FAQ and a real final CTA.
  5. Hero headline said nothing. "Every screen. One Moonlit." does not tell a new visitor what Moonlit is. The new headline and three meta lines cover price, ads and devices above the fold.
  6. Pricing copy disagreed with itself. Landing said 6 profiles and 4K, the pricing page said 5 profiles and HD. One set of plan facts now, ready to reuse on the pricing page.
  7. Device list disagreed with the Download page. Landing listed Web, iOS, Mac; Download lists Mac, iOS, Apple TV, Windows. The devices section now matches the Download page, with a status pill for each.
  8. Dead links. "About" and "Status" in the footer went to "#". Removed.
  9. No call to action on phones. The navbar hid Sign in and Get Moonlit below 640px. The primary button now stays visible at every width.
  10. Content hidden until scroll. Reveal-on-scroll left sections at opacity 0, so previews, printouts and slow connections showed blank gaps. Everything renders at rest.
  11. Uneven rhythm. Sections alternated between huge padding and cramped cards. One spacing scale now.
  12. Card on everything. Steps, stats, platforms and plans were all identical bordered boxes. Only plans and mockups keep the card treatment.

Suggested next step

  • Pick a concept. I port it into the React landing page, Navbar, Pricing and Download pages, and reuse the plan facts from one shared file so they cannot drift again.
